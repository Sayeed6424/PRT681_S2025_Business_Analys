**Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs)**



Objective: Strengthen understanding of NFRs and how they shape system quality.



Detailed Learnings:



Functional vs Non-Functional:



Functional = system does something specific.



Non-Functional = system meets quality standards while doing it.



Categories of NFRs:



Performance: response times, throughput, scalability.



Security: authentication, encryption, access control.



Usability: intuitive design, accessibility (WCAG compliance).



Reliability/Availability: uptime % (e.g., 99.9% SLA).



Compliance: GDPR, ISO 27001, PCI DSS.



Applied Exercise: Drafted 5 NFRs for a login \& payment system:



Login page must load within 2 seconds under 1,000 concurrent users.



Passwords must be stored with SHA-256 encryption.



Account is locked after 5 failed login attempts.



Payment system must have 99.9% uptime monthly.



Payment process must comply with PCI DSS standards.
