**Video – How BAs and Data Analysts Collaborate in Projects**



Objective: Understand real-world collaboration between BA and DA roles.



Detailed Learnings:



Key Roles:



BA defines business problem, gathers requirements, ensures stakeholder needs are captured.



DA explores data sources, runs analysis, and presents insights.



Collaboration Workflow:



BA frames the question: e.g., “Which region’s sales are underperforming compared to last year?”



DA queries \& analyzes data: Uses SQL/Power BI to provide evidence.



BA interprets insights with stakeholders: Translates DA’s charts into business recommendations.



Three Collaboration Benefits:



Shared understanding reduces misalignment.



Speeds up decision-making by combining BA context + DA analysis.



Improves solution design (BA ensures requirements, DA ensures feasibility).
